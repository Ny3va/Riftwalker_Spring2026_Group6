Riftwalker : Transformation Phase : Group 6

Unity is an engine that is built for both 3D and 2D cross-platform development. It provides a visual environment to build scenes and configure behavior by adding components. Features of unity that increase its ease of use are the physics engine, C# scripting with easy creation and access, cross platform support that allows for deployment on many different platforms, and more. 


Instructions:

- The WASD keys on the keyboard allow the player to move forward, left, backward, and right.
- The spacebar allows the player to jump a short distance in the air.
- The mouse allows for movement of the camera to look around and investigate the surroundings. 

- Left click ejects a projectile which will create a rift when it collides with a viable surface.
[Only one rift can exist on the map at a time, if there is an attempt to place a second rift the original will disappear]

- The E key allows for the interaction of objects such as doors or cubes. When the player is within a certain distance of an interactable object, they can press the E key to interact with it. This can include opening doors, picking up keys, or activating switches.


Map Features:

- Some doors are functional and can be opened by the player, while others are locked and require a key to open. Keys can be found somewhere the map and may be hidden in crates or behind objects.
- Maps are designed similar but with slight differences and puzzles to solve. Maps require the player to jump between worlds to solve puzzles and reach the exit door.


The game will start with the menu to where currently the player can start the game and quit. In the future, there will be options to change settings and view credits.
